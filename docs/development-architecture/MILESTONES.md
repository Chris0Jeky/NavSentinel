# Milestone contracts

Milestones are outcome gates, not folders for subsystems. Labels and the issue
map carry domain, gate, evidence, and work-state details. No milestone receives
a speculative due date.

## M0 — `M0-proving-ground-foundation`

State: **active**. Test and evidence infrastructure only.

Outcome: hermetic attack, benign, and mixed journeys use trusted input, typed
loopback sinks, independent harm oracles, deterministic mutation, and exact-head
receipts.

Exit evidence:

- core proof never equates an event, toast, transient DOM state, or rollback
  with protection;
- held external destinations are localised or retired;
- representative overlay, navigation, credential, ClickFix, and
  DoubleClickjacking families have attack/benign/mixed contracts;
- valid survivors persist and lower evidence automatically;
- receipts identify commit, artifact, browser, input, sink, outcome, and limits;
- supported Windows and hosted browser topology fails deterministically.

## M1 — `v0.5.0-unlisted-beta`

State: **active**. Only release-integrity, privacy, consent, authority, bridge,
package/store truth, and owner-gate blockers belong here.

Exit evidence:

- protection-lowering actions are extension-origin, exact-context bound,
  one-shot, and short-lived;
- bridge trust, liveness, starvation, recovery, and failure policy are explicit;
- fresh installs are passive until informed activation and revocation is proven;
- persistent/session data is purpose-minimised and the declared behavioural
  reset boundary is complete;
- permission, privacy, package, store, and claims truth match the exact
  interaction-only artifact;
- owner Chrome and external-review prerequisites remain explicit.

## M2 — `v0.5.1-interaction-integrity`

State: **planned**. Starts after the M1 exact release head is stable, except for
a bounded regression repair required by M1.

Exit evidence: exact navigation authority cannot be double-spent; overlay
suppression has bounded ownership and reversible groups; recovery remains
accessible; settings use coherent patch/revision semantics; latency and
interruption have declared budgets and benign controls.

## M3 — `v0.6.0-local-evidence-plane`

State: **planned**. Schema/read-model work waits for M1 data boundaries.

Exit evidence: stable event/outcome identity and page attribution; one bounded
sanitised source model feeding current-page, journal, review, Protection Center,
and Data Flow Lens projections; corrections remain separate facts; export is
previewed, allowlisted, size-bounded, and secret-free.

## M4 — `EV-1-efficacy-and-quietness`

State: **planned**, with #417 methodology allowed to start immediately.
Detector/scoring changes remain measurement-gated.

Exit evidence: corpus v2 uses faithful origins and trusted input; named benign
journeys measure interruption and breakage; outcomes distinguish pre-harm block,
hold, warning, rollback, recovery, harm, invalid test, and no signal; branded
Chrome is used for Safe Browsing comparisons; every tuning change reports attack,
benign, latency, holdout, sample-size, and confidence deltas.

## M5 — `BETA-1-cohort-and-operations`

State: **gated** until M1 closes and an exact unlisted artifact is installable.

Exit evidence: 15 invitations, 10 activated daily-use installs, 7/10 enabled at
D14 and 6/10 at D30, reasons for every non-install/disable/uninstall, operated
support/vulnerability/rollback paths, and external-review findings resolved or
explicitly accepted before public launch. No silent telemetry is introduced.

## `maintenance-icebox`

State: **passive**. An item leaves only when an active milestone touches the same
seam or the maintainer explicitly promotes it. It is never an autonomous fallback.

## R1 — `post-beta-horizon`

State: **frozen**. A research option moves out only with a falsifiable experiment,
privacy/permission boundary, maintenance owner, kill criterion, and explicit owner
promotion after retention/evidence.

## Closure rule

A milestone closes only when its exit evidence is true, every residual is moved,
accepted, or rejected, the roadmap/security-programme state agrees, and the exact
artifact or evidence receipt is identifiable. Closing all issues is not enough.
