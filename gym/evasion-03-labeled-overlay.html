Evasion 03: Misleading accessible label

The overlays use deceptive aria-labels: one with a single whitespace character (aria-label=" "), the other with a single letter (aria-label="x"). The CDS hardening treats labels shorter than 2 characters as absent, so both should score as no_accessible_name.

This is an isolated single-signal test: no opacity tricks, no high z-index, no large coverage. It verifies that the label-stripping logic works, though it will not block on its own (CDS ~15, below both thresholds).
