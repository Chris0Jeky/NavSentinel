Evasion 12 - Multiple Foreground Overlays

Attack scenario: Two unnamed, near-transparent navigation overlays are already stacked over the intended link when the page becomes interactive. Hiding only the top layer exposes a second trap.

Expected with automatic cleanup enabled: NavSentinel hides both bounded high-risk candidates without opening a tab, exposes the real link, and offers one Undo action that restores both overlays.

Expected with cleanup disabled: The overlays remain present; the normal click-time navigation guard is still the fallback.
