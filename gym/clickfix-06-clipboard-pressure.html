ClickFix 06 - Clipboard pressure before the bridge handshake

Scenario: a page floods the clipboard while Heedline is still starting up. As soon as the MAIN-world clipboard hook is installed, and before the bridge reports ready, it writes 40 harmless labels. The attack arm then writes one inert command-like value last.

Expected: the benign arm stays silent; the attack arm shows the fake-verification clipboard warning; neither arm adds a bridge_buffer_overflow event. Without the #599 queue policy the flood fills the pre-handshake queue, the command-like receipt is dropped, and an overflow row appears.

Setup: Chrome writes to the clipboard without a click only when this site's Clipboard permission is Allow (Site settings). Use ?mode=benign or ?mode=attack; the self-check below must read VALID for the result to count.
