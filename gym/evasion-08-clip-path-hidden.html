Evasion 08: clip-path visual hiding

The overlay uses clip-path: inset(0 0 98% 0) to clip itself to only a thin strip at the top of the viewport. In Chrome, clip-path affects hit-testing: clicks outside the clipped area pass through.

This documents that clip-path is not a viable evasion for full-viewport overlays, but could be used for targeted thin-strip overlays that cover navigation bars or specific UI elements.
