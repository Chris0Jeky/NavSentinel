DoubleClickjacking 04 - Payment Button Variant

Attack pattern: A checkout page asks the user to double-click the "Pay Now" button. On the first click, a child window opens and navigates the opener to a different payment confirmation page using window.opener.location. The second click lands on the attacker's payment page, authorizing an unintended transaction.

Expected: NavSentinel detects the DoubleClickjacking pattern and flags the second click with nrs_double_click_hijack.
