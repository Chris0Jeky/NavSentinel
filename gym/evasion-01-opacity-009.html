Evasion 01: Opacity 0.09 (near-invisible)

The overlay uses opacity 0.09 — just above the 0.08 invisibility threshold. This tests whether the gradient penalty in near_invisible_opacity catches nearly-invisible elements that technically pass the isVisible() check.

This is an isolated single-signal test: the overlay is small and uses low z-index to avoid triggering other penalties. It verifies that the near_invisible_opacity gradient works, but won't block on its own.
