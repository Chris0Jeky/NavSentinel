NavSentinel

Catches what Safe Browsing can't see. NavSentinel detects interaction-level browser attacks — DoubleClickjacking, ClickFix, redirect chains, and OAuth abuse — without sending data anywhere. Everything runs locally.

Detection layers

How it works

Monitors clicks

Watches for suspicious overlay patterns, hidden elements, and double-click hijacking that trick you into unintended actions.

Analyzes navigation

Scores navigation risk — cross-site jumps, redirect chains, and known-bad domains all raise the risk level.

Protects credentials

Warns about credential entry on suspicious or lookalike domains before your password leaves the browser.

Notifications

What to expect

When NavSentinel detects something suspicious, you'll see a prompt. You always choose whether to allow or block.

Caution
Suspicious navigation to example-login.site. This may be a deceptive redirect.
Allow once Block
Blocked

Protection modes

Choose your level

Smart

Balanced protection. Prompts on suspicious cross-site navigations and credential risks. Recommended for most users.

Strict

Maximum protection. Prompts on all cross-site navigations and blocks risky credential submissions.

Off

Monitoring only. Events are logged but nothing is blocked or prompted. Useful for testing.

Change modes anytime from the popup or the options dashboard.
