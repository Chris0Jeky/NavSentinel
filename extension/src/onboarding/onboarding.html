NavSentinel

Catches what Safe Browsing can't see. NavSentinel detects interaction-level browser attacks — DoubleClickjacking, ClickFix, redirect chains, and OAuth abuse — without sending data anywhere. Everything runs locally in your browser.

NS
Detection layers

How it works

Monitors clicks

Watches for suspicious overlay patterns, hidden elements, and double-click hijacking that trick you into clicking something you didn't intend.

Analyzes navigation

Scores navigation risk based on context — cross-site jumps, redirect chains, and known-bad domains all raise the risk level.

Protects credentials

Warns about credential entry on suspicious or lookalike domains before your password leaves the browser.

Notifications

What to expect

When NavSentinel detects something suspicious, you'll see a notification. You can always choose to allow the navigation.

Protection modes

Choose your level

Smart

Balanced protection. Prompts on suspicious cross-site navigations and credential risks. Recommended for most users.

Strict

Maximum protection. Prompts on all cross-site navigations and blocks risky credential submissions by default.

Off

Monitoring only. Events are logged but no navigations are blocked or prompted. Useful for testing.

You can change modes anytime from the popup or the options page.
